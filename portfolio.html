Personal project

Spoon Tracker

  • Calendar analysis
  • Building with AI

I built an energy tracker for myself with AI writing the code, then used it every day for 90 days. When I checked it against my real calendar, the app couldn’t see a third of my life, so I added the missing activities without breaking a single logged entry. I’m still iterating on it, making it easier to use and more useful about what my data says.
